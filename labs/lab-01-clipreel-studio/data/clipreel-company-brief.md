## 1. Mission

> Publish 8 short-form videos per month across TikTok, Reels and Shorts at an average
> watch-through rate of at least 5%.

This is the mission you will later enter into Paperclip. Read it as three parts, because a
mission that cannot be broken this way cannot be verified:

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Target | 8 short-form videos per month |
| Metric | Average watch-through rate |
| Boundary | At least 5%, across all three channels |

A mission such as "grow the channel" has a target of nothing, a metric of nothing and a boundary
of nothing. It would pass a wizard's validation and fail every audit afterwards.

## 2. What ClipReel Studio does

ClipReel Studio is a one-person short-video production company operating out of a home studio in
Singapore. The founder writes, films and edits vertical video for small local brands — cafés,
fitness studios, tuition centres — and publishes on the founder's own channels as a showcase.

The bottleneck is not filming. It is the research-and-scripting front half of the pipeline:
finding a trend early enough to matter, and turning it into a script that survives the first
three seconds. That front half is exactly the part a small agent company can carry.

## 5. The three roles

ClipReel Studio's agent company has three roles. You do not create them in Lab 1 — Lab 1 proves
the control plane only — but they are the shape everything later builds towards.

### 5.1 Head of Content (CEO)

- **Purpose.** Owns the mission, decomposes it into work, and synthesises the final publishing
  plan from the specialists' artifacts.
- **Reports to.** Nobody — top of the org chart.
- **Owns.** Goal alignment, the monthly slate, final sign-off before publishing.
- **Never does.** Filming, editing, or publishing directly to a live channel.

### 5.2 Trend Researcher

- **Purpose.** Identifies emerging formats, sounds and hooks relevant to the ClipReel niches, and
  reports them with a stated confidence and a stated limitation.
- **Reports to.** Head of Content.
- **Produces.** A findings table — claim, source label, confidence, limitation.
- **Never does.** Write scripts, or assert a trend without naming what it could not establish.

### 5.3 Script Writer

- **Purpose.** Turns an accepted trend finding into a shootable script with a hook, a body and a
  call to action, sized to the target channel.
- **Reports to.** Head of Content.
- **Produces.** One script file per commissioned video, with the target channel and target
  duration stated at the top.
- **Never does.** Source its own trends — it consumes the Trend Researcher's accepted artifact.

## 6. Why this brief exists

Lab 1 asks you to copy this file into the practice root before you create anything in Paperclip.
That is deliberate. The mission comes first, the control plane is proved second, and only then is
anybody hired. A company that gains agents before it gains a verified platform and a written
mission has no way of telling a healthy run from an expensive one.
